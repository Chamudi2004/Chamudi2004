## 🚀 About Me

I am a CIS undergraduate at Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka, passionate about transforming complex problems into seamless, user-centric digital solutions. My expertise lies at the dynamic intersection of **mobile application development**, **frontend engineering**, and **data analysis**. 

- 📱 **Mobile:** Crafting robust, cross-platform apps with Flutter, and Dart.
- 💻 **Frontend:** Building responsive, modern web UIs using React.js, Tailwind CSS, and HTML5/CSS3.
- ⚙️ **Backend & Data:** Structuring NoSQL databases, managing datasets, and building APIs with Python and FastAPI.
- 🌱 **Currently exploring:** Advanced AI LLM integrations (like Gemini) and real-time AIoT cloud architectures.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** UI/UX prototyping, Flutter state management, or data-driven systems.
